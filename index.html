(01) The Studio

Interiors with intention.

We design homes that balance architecture, functionality and atmosphere — creating spaces that feel timeless, personal and distinctly yours.

Every FORMA interior begins with the architecture: proportion, light and the way you move through a room. Materials come next — chosen for how they age, not only how they photograph.

(05) Materials

The Material Palette

Six families of honest, natural materials — sourced for their character, detailed to age gracefully and chosen to be touched every day.

  • Wood

    European white oak and American walnut, oiled by hand.

  • Stone

    Honed travertine and limestone with their natural pores left open.

  • Marble

    Calacatta and Nero Marquina, book-matched and lightly polished.

  • Metal

    Brushed bronze, satin brass and blackened steel details.

  • Glass

    Low-iron, fluted and smoked glass to shape light and privacy.

  • Fabric

    Bouclé, washed linen and vegetable-tanned leather.

(07) About FORMA

We design how home feels.

FORMA began in 2010 as a two-person studio in Kochi with a simple belief: a home should be shaped around the people inside it, not around trends.

Today we are a team of architects, interior designers and makers working across India and the Gulf. We still sketch every project by hand, still visit every stone yard, and still sit with our clients long before a single line is drawn.

  • Architecture first

    Proportion, light and circulation come before furniture.

  • Honest materials

    Natural materials, detailed to age with grace.

  • Made to be lived in

    Beauty that survives children, guests and time.
