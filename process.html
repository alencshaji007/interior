Our Process

A Clear, Coordinated Approach

Successful project delivery starts with understanding the brief and continues through careful planning, communication and execution.

Six stages

From Your Vision to Your Finished Space

Every project is different. We tailor the process to the property's requirements, the agreed scope and any applicable authority or building-management procedures.

  2. 01

    Discover

    We discuss your goals, the property, functional requirements, budget considerations and design preferences.

  3. 02

    Design

    We develop the proposed layout, interior concept and design details appropriate to the agreed brief.

  4. 03

    Define

    We finalise the agreed scope, material specifications, quotation and execution requirements.

  5. 04

    Coordinate

    We coordinate relevant approvals, technical details, suppliers, joinery and work schedules as required.

  6. 05

    Execute

    We manage the agreed site works and coordinate the different trades to bring the approved design to life.

  7. 06

    Handover

    We review the completed scope, address agreed outstanding items and coordinate the handover.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. We manage the project from design development through to complete turnkey execution, based on the agreed scope.

Do you manufacture your own joinery?

Our joinery is produced at the Ajman factory run by JAN Interiors, which focuses exclusively on joinery production.

Do you assist with approvals and NOCs?

Yes. We coordinate applicable building-management and authority approvals, subject to project-specific requirements.

What types of properties do you work on?

Offices, villas, apartments and warehouses or commercial facilities.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, size and approvals. We provide a clear timeline with every quotation.
